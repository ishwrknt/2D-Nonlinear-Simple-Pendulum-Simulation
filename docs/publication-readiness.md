# Publication-readiness assessment

The revised simulation is a locally tested release candidate for physics education use. It is not itself a submitted or accepted paper.

## Supported by this release

- Corrected nonlinear angular/Cartesian calculations with damping/driving and continuous rotations.
- Independent finite-trajectory, period, convergence, energy and constraint checks with reproducible scripts and actual results.
- Student investigations and teacher prompts, parameter units, selectable plots/data, reproducible setups and exports.
- Source/version provenance, documented numerical limits and SMART correction cycle.

## Required before journal submission

- [ ] Revise the writable manuscript to describe version 0.3.0. Its old numerical results refer to the original implementation; do not silently replace or combine them.
- [ ] Separate original development history from this SMART revision and disclose actual AI assistance. Preserve the approved author list: Ishwar Kant, O. S. K. S. Sastri, M. R. Ganesh Kumar; confirm affiliations and author approval.
- [ ] Select/confirm the journal and verify its current scope, formatting, disclosure and data/code policies directly. Earlier preference was Physics Education at physedu.in; do not assume it means a different journal with the same name.
- [ ] Authors should inspect equations, proposed activities and figures, test intended classroom scenarios, and decide how real-apparatus comparison or a classroom pilot would strengthen the education contribution.
- [ ] Support learning-effectiveness claims with a suitably designed educational study if those claims are made. No such study is included.
- [ ] Broaden validation if the article claims accuracy beyond these parameter/time ranges, especially sensitive driven regimes or extreme controls.
- [ ] Review license/redistribution terms for the simulation source and choose an explicit release license where absent; do not silently assign one.
- [ ] After review, merge/deploy the approved version, verify the public URL, and archive the exact source/data version with a persistent identifier if required.
- [ ] Cite authentic scholarly/software references supporting actual statements; do not cite local draft PDFs as published research.

The updated source is a basis for a defensible methods/education article. Numerical correctness alone does not establish novelty, educational effectiveness, or journal acceptance. Original source files on the shared volume and existing manuscript content were not overwritten during this simulation revision.

Fluid extension: any article including air/water/oil scenarios must identify the quasi-steady drag approximation, its sources, illustrative properties, buoyancy potential and omitted added-mass/unsteady effects. Numerical reference agreement is not experimental validation of real liquid motion. Do not infer real oil behaviour from density alone.
