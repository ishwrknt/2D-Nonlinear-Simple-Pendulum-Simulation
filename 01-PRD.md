# Requirements
- Point mass and rigid rod, fixed pivot, vertical plane; disclose assumptions.
- Full nonlinear restoring torque; linear/quadratic damping and sinusoidal drive in both coordinate solvers.
- Continuous angle, correctly signed velocity, interpolated crossings, conditional theoretical predictions.
- Units, editable parameters, presets, pause/reset/one-step, dragging and playback.
- K/U/E and transfer plots, phase and coordinate plots, useful live data, CSV with metadata, shared setups.
- Responsive keyboard-accessible controls and teacher activities.
- Independent reference checks, convergence, constraints, energy balance and browser validation.
- Build and repeatable release evidence; no invented observations or classroom outcomes.
